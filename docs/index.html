How it works

Greenhouse · Lever · Ashby · Workday→ free title filter→ Haiku: relevant?→ Opus or Haiku: fit 0–100→ Notion row + 3 bullets

The free regex pass drops about 98% of postings before any model call. A seen-cache means the same job is never scored twice. Every run has a hard dollar cap and logs its cost.

Why not an auto-apply tool?

You stay in control

Matches land as "Not Applied". Every application is one you read, chose, and can defend in an interview.

No account risk

No bot in your LinkedIn session, no stored passwords. Only public job-board APIs that exist for exactly this.

No invented experience

Bullets may use only facts from your resume. A judge model audits every draft and flags anything unsupported.

Measured, not vibes

A pytest eval suite grades the scorer against hand labels: precision 0.78, recall 0.83 on the author's set.

Questions

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, and it never will. It finds, scores, and drafts. You apply. Mass auto-apply doubles the pile every recruiter has to read and gets accounts restricted.

What do I need?

A GitHub account (free), a Notion account (free), and a Claude API key from platform.claude.com with a spend limit you choose. The guide walks through all three.

Where does my resume go?

Into a GitHub Actions secret in your own repo. From there to the Claude API and to your Notion workspace. Nowhere else. The code is public, so you can check.

Which job boards?

Any company that publishes jobs through Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or Workday, which is most of tech and a lot of healthcare, finance and government contractors. 235 boards are verified in the box; adding one is one line of YAML.

I'm not in QA. Will it work for me?

Yes. The lane packs cover data, software, DevOps, IT, product and UX, and the scoring guidance is a few bullets you write in plain English about how you decide.

Refunds?

If it does not run for you within 14 days and the troubleshooting guide did not help, ask and you get your money back.
